A single line that makes office life funny, written for the [One-Line Office Jokes](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/05vf2eby).

The program writes one joke to `/output/entry.txt`. The Monday meeting could have been an email, but the calendar needed a victim.

## Entry

> The Monday meeting could have been an email, but the calendar needed a victim, so nine of us are now nodding at a slide nobody read.

The joke turns on a real detail of office life, the Monday meeting, and lands the laugh in the last few words.
